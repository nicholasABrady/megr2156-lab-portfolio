#About Me

I have always been interested in cars, starting from kindergarten. My every waking moment was spent thinking about cars, watching Top Gear, or playing racing games. Eventually, this translated into a love for racing. Even as a child, I had an interest in NASCAR and Formula 1 but I never paid enough attention to understand them. In freshman year of high school, I randomly decided to follow my childhood dream of becoming a NASCAR driver. I did all the research, trying to figure out how to launch my career. Unfortunately, Delaware is not a sports hotspot let alone the greatest place for racing; that alongside my complete lack of experience told me I should change my route. Having been proficient at math and science growing up, I felt that engineering was the best path for me to get into the motorsports field. At this point, I had some experience in researching and analyzing the design of vehicles. 

At about the same time as my NASCAR dream, my dad bought me two old Kawasaki dirtbikes. I taught myself how to ride a motorcycle while spending days over the summer trying to get one of the bikes to run. This was my first hands-on project and it taught me a lot about planning your work before you execute it. I removed, tweaked, and replaced many parts on that dirtbike that now I know were completely unnecessary to. If I had a chance to redo that "refurbishment", I would do a little more research and be much more careful before trying to clean a carburetor with water (all that did was swell the gaskets, ensuring the bike would never run).
